Semiconductor fabs run hundreds of process steps on shared, re-entrant tool groups, so work-in-process tends to pile up unevenly across the line. This project studies how to keep the **workload flowing in balance** through the fab by deciding how **tool capacity is allocated** across process steps over&nbsp;time.

- **Feedback control:** capacity allocation is adjusted continuously from the observed workload state, steering the line back toward balance instead of relying on a fixed&nbsp;plan.
- **Reinforcement learning:** the control policy is adapted by reinforcement learning so that it keeps up with changing product mix and fab&nbsp;conditions.
